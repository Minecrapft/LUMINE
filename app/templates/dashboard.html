First time? Here’s the simple version.

Open each step for a helpful tip.

  1. 1 Get the strip ready

    Follow the timing on the test package. Place the strip on a clean, flat surface.

  2. 2 Keep the whole strip in view

    Fit both ends and every color pad inside the camera guide. Use steady light and avoid shadows or glare.

  3. 3 Review what LUMINE noticed

    LUMINE compares colors with saved references. It is a screening aid, not a diagnosis. Ask a health professional about results that need review.

  4. 4 Choose what to do next

    If a result recommends follow-up, talk with a qualified health professional. If LUMINE could not read the photo, follow the prompts to try again.

i

A helpful check, not a diagnosis. LUMINE cannot replace a lab test or advice from a qualified health professional.
